EDUCATION

E-School

E-School connects school administrators, teachers, students and parents so school operations and each learner's progress can be managed in one place.

  • School administration and academic records
  • Assessments, grades, teacher remarks and progress tracking
  • Parent access and communication with the school

A platform in development, with a public website for schools to explore its proposed services.

HEALTHCARE

SaloneCare

SaloneCare is our healthcare platform concept for a more connected digital health system in Sierra Leone.

  • Connected patient records and care histories
  • Medical data analysis to support care planning
  • Clinical decision and prescription support for qualified healthcare professionals

At concept and planning stage. Clinical features require professional oversight and validation before use in patient care.
