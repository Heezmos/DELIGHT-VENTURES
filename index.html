About Us

One Holding Company. A Shared Ambition.

We bring focused ventures together around a common goal: making essential services more accessible through enterprise and digital innovation.

Delight Holdings Limited carries forward the identity and foundation of Delight Ventures Limited as the umbrella for EZGo, E-School, Kwik Pay, Ecofarms and SaloneCare. Each venture retains its own purpose within our shared vision.

Our portfolio spans mobility, education, payments, agriculture and healthcare. Alongside these ventures, we support entrepreneurs and organizations with business consultancy, compliance support and digital systems.

We are developing these ventures step by step, with shared planning and technical expertise. Our focus is practical delivery, sustainable growth and long-term value for Sierra Leone and African markets.

Our Mission

To build and support ventures that improve access to essential services, strengthen businesses and create lasting value through digital innovation.

Our Vision

To grow a trusted African group of companies, starting in Sierra Leone, that connects enterprise, technology and opportunities for shared progress.
